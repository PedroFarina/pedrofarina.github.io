Common questions

  • How do I share a household? Open the household's settings and tap Invite Member. People join through iCloud sharing, so everyone needs to be signed in to iCloud.
  • My changes don't show up on another device. Check that both devices are signed in to the same iCloud account (or, for a shared household, that the invitation was accepted), that iCloud is turned on for Tidyo (Settings › your name › iCloud), and that the device is online.
  • How do I cancel Tidyo+? In App Settings › Manage Subscription, or in your Apple Account settings. You keep Tidyo+ until the end of the period you've paid for.
  • What happens if my subscription ends? Nothing is deleted. Households you own become read-only until you renew.
  • How do I delete my data? App Settings › Erase All App Data removes everything from the device and from iCloud.
