Privacy Policy

Effective October 21, 2026

The short version
  • We don't collect your data. Tidyo has no accounts and no servers of its own, and we can't see anything you put in it.
  • Your households, chores and grocery lists are kept on your devices and in your own iCloud account. When you share a household, its members can see it too.
  • There's no tracking, no advertising, no analytics and no third-party code in Tidyo.
  • Smart suggestions, translation and scanning all run on your device.
  • You can delete everything at any time with Erase All App Data.

This policy explains how the Tidyo app for iPhone, iPad and Mac ("Tidyo", "the app") handles information. Tidyo is made by Pedro Farina ("we", "us"). When this policy says "collect", it means data leaving your device in a way that lets us or anyone else other than you, the people you share with, and Apple acting as your iCloud and App Store provider, access it.

What Tidyo stores, and where

Tidyo keeps what you create in it:

  • household names, and the people in each household;
  • your name as you enter it in Tidyo (it starts as your device type, such as "iPhone", until you change it) and the languages you write in;
  • chores, their schedules, who they're assigned to, and when they were done or skipped;
  • grocery lists, items, shopping sessions, and a history of what was bought.

This data is stored on your device. If you're signed in to iCloud, Tidyo also keeps a copy in your own private iCloud database (Apple's CloudKit) so it stays in step across your devices. That copy lives in your iCloud account, under Apple's privacy policy and your iCloud settings. We have no access to it, and we don't run any server that could hold a copy.

If you aren't signed in to iCloud, everything stays on the device.

Sharing a household

Households are shared through iCloud sharing, the same mechanism Apple's Notes and Reminders use. When you invite someone, or accept an invitation:

  • everyone in the household can see and change that household's chores, grocery lists and history, and can see each member's Tidyo name and languages;
  • the household's owner can see who has joined. iCloud may show the name, email address or phone number of each person's Apple Account so the owner can tell who is who, under Apple's iCloud sharing rules;
  • the household's data is stored in the owner's iCloud account and shared with the members. It isn't sent to us.

Only invite people you want to share the household with. The owner can remove members or stop sharing at any time, and any member can leave.

Your Tidyo+ subscription status is shared with your household's members as a single date (when your access is covered until), so their devices know whether the household is fully editable. Nothing else about your purchase is shared.

Features that run on your device

Smart suggestions

Where your device supports Apple Intelligence, Tidyo can suggest a schedule for a chore, a category and unit for a grocery item, turn notes or a recipe into grocery items, fill in a Chores First rule from a description, and point out likely duplicates. These use Apple's on-device language model. Your text is processed on your device and isn't sent anywhere. Each suggestion is only applied if you choose it, and each feature can be turned off in App Settings.

Translation

Tidyo can translate chore and grocery text written in another language into yours. It uses Apple's Translation framework, which translates on your device; your device may download language files from Apple the first time a language is used. Working out which language a text is in uses Apple's on-device Natural Language framework. You can turn translation off in App Settings.

Scanning lists and receipts

If you scan a paper list or a receipt, Tidyo uses the camera or a photo you pick, and reads the text with Apple's on-device text recognition. The image is only used to read the text and isn't kept or sent anywhere. Tidyo asks for camera access only when you start a scan, and only sees the photos you pick.

Chores First and Screen Time

Chores First lets you choose apps to block until your chores are done. It uses Apple's Screen Time frameworks (Family Controls), with your permission, for your own device only. The apps you pick are given to Tidyo as opaque tokens it can't read; Tidyo can't see which apps they are, how you use them, or any other Screen Time data. Your Chores First rules are stored on that device only. They aren't synced to iCloud or shared with your household, and they're removed by Erase All App Data.

Notifications, widgets, Live Activities, Siri and Reminders

  • Notifications. Chore reminders and updates about your household (such as a member finishing the shopping) are created on your device from your Tidyo data. Tidyo receives silent iCloud notifications that tell it when there are changes to sync; these carry no content of your own.
  • Widgets and Live Activities. They show a summary of your chores and lists, stored on your device for the widgets to read.
  • Siri, Shortcuts and App Intents. The names of your grocery lists and chores are made available to Siri and Shortcuts on your device so you can refer to them by name. Requests you make to Siri are handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy.
  • Reminders. If you turn on Sync to Reminders, Tidyo adds your chores to a "Tidyo" list in Apple's Reminders app, with your permission. That list then follows your own Reminders and iCloud settings.

Purchases

Tidyo+ subscriptions are bought, renewed, cancelled and refunded through Apple's App Store. Apple handles your payment details; we never see them. The app asks the App Store whether your subscription is active, and we receive the usual sales reports from Apple, which don't identify you. If you share Tidyo+ through Family Sharing, Apple handles that too.

Diagnostics

Tidyo uses Apple's MetricKit to receive performance and crash summaries, which it writes only to your device's own log. It doesn't send them anywhere. If you've chosen to share analytics with app developers in your device's settings, Apple may share crash reports and usage statistics with us in an aggregated form under Apple's privacy policy. You can change that in Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements.

What we don't do

  • We don't track you across apps or websites, and Tidyo doesn't ask to.
  • There are no ads, no analytics, no third-party SDKs and no data brokers.
  • We don't sell or share your data, because we don't have it.
  • Tidyo doesn't use your location or your contacts.

Deleting your data

  • Erase All App Data (App Settings) deletes every household, chore and grocery list from the device and from your iCloud, stops sharing the households you own, leaves the ones shared with you, and removes Tidyo's widgets data, reminders, Reminders list and Chores First rules. If the device is offline, iCloud is updated once it reconnects.
  • Leaving a household removes you from it. Deleting a household you own removes it for every member.
  • You can also delete Tidyo's iCloud data from iCloud settings (Settings › your name › iCloud › Manage Storage on iPhone). Deleting the app removes the data stored on that device.

Data another member added to a household they own stays with that household.

Children

Tidyo is made for households and isn't directed at children under 13. Because we don't collect personal information from anyone, we don't knowingly collect it from children either. A parent who wants a child's Tidyo data deleted can use the steps above on the child's device, or contact us.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or move your personal data. Because your Tidyo data is in your own devices and iCloud account, you can use these rights directly in the app and in iCloud. We hold nothing about you to give back or delete, but you're welcome to contact us with any question.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the effective date above. If a change affects how your data is handled, we'll tell you in the app or in the App Store release notes before it takes effect.
