3. When a subscription ends

If your Tidyo+ subscription or trial ends, nothing is deleted. The households you own become read-only for everyone in them: you can still open and read everything, but not add or change chores and lists until the subscription is renewed. A shopping trip that's already under way can still be finished. Even while read-only you can always leave a household, delete one you own, remove its members, and erase all of Tidyo's data.

A household that someone else owns follows that person's subscription, not yours.

4. Shared households

A household is shared through iCloud with the people its owner invites. Everyone in a household can see and change what's in it. When you use a shared household, you agree to:

  • invite only people you know and want to share with, and keep invitation links to yourself;
  • respect the other members and what they add, and not use Tidyo to harass, threaten or embarrass anyone;
  • not add anything unlawful, or anything you don't have the right to share;
  • not use Tidyo in a way that interferes with the app, iCloud, or other people's use of them.

The owner of a household decides who is in it and can remove members or stop sharing at any time. Any member can leave. We can't see, moderate or recover the contents of a household; what members put in it is their responsibility.
